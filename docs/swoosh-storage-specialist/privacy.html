Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Chrome extension Swoosh Cookie and Local Storage Specialist handles user data and ensures your privacy.

2. Data Collection

Swoosh Cookie and Local Storage Specialist does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All operations — including reading, editing, or deleting Cookies, Local Storage, and Session Storage — occur locally within your browser.

3. Data Usage

The extension uses Chrome’s built-in APIs (such as cookies, storage, and activeTab) solely to perform local operations. No information is sent to external servers.

4. Analytics and Tracking

This extension does not include analytics, advertising, telemetry, or any form of user tracking.

5. Third-Party Access

No third parties have access to the data managed by this extension. All data remains fully under the user’s control.

6. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store support page .
